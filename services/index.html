Software and services

Choose who runs Pmitz

Use the freely licensed software with your own team, or discuss a Pmitz-operated SaaS subscription. Both choices use the same product.

Your team operates it

Pmitz’s published software is licensed under Apache 2.0, with no software license fee. Your team can integrate, configure, test, and operate it in your own environment.

Your team owns hosting, updates, maintenance, storage, and the associated operating costs. A free software license does not make those tasks free.

Start with the software or read the Apache 2.0 license .

Pmitz operates it for you

The operated-service offering is a paid Pmitz SaaS subscription, with hosting, updates, maintenance, and professional services or support within agreed terms. You can discuss this option from the start or after operating the software yourself.

Agree the service scope

Talk to Terpomo about availability, pricing, service hours, supported versions, included integration work, backup and recovery, and service levels. The presentation does not establish a published SaaS price, a response-time guarantee, or security certification.

Moving an existing installation may require planning, data transfer, configuration changes, integration work, and testing. It is not an automatic migration, and not every extension or data item is guaranteed to transfer without work.

How Pmitz fits alongside billing

Your billing provider can continue collecting payments, producing invoices, and managing the relevant commercial subscription information. Your application supplies that information to its Pmitz integration where needed.

Pmitz focuses on configured feature access, applicable customer limits, and reported usage in the application. This is a choice of product and operating model, not a claim that those capabilities are unique to Pmitz.

Stripe offers billing-linked entitlements , and Chargebee provides feature and entitlement management . Specialist products such as Stigg also address runtime entitlement and usage workflows. Evaluate fit, integration, operating responsibilities, and commercial terms for your own requirements.

Pmitz does not imply a shipped Stripe or Chargebee connector, a nontechnical policy editor, or a usage analytics dashboard.

A focused first conversation

Bring an existing feature, a current plan, a changing limit, and the way you identify users or customers. A useful evaluation connects one feature, checks permitted and declined requests, reports successful use, changes a plan rule, and gathers your team’s feedback.
