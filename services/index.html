Pmitz SaaS

Terpomo Software operates Pmitz for your team under a SaaS subscription. Hosting, updates, maintenance, and agreed professional services or support are handled by the company behind the product. Your team continues to own and run your application.

Your subscription and your customers’ plans

Your Pmitz SaaS subscription is an agreement with Terpomo Software to operate Pmitz. Your customers separately buy your application’s plans. Pmitz applies the feature and usage rules associated with those plans; your billing system continues to collect payments and produce invoices.

Discuss availability, pricing, service hours, supported versions, included professional services, backup and recovery, and service levels with Terpomo Software.

Moving an existing deployment can involve data transfer, configuration changes, extension work, and testing. Migration scope and responsibilities are agreed as part of the work.

Run Pmitz yourself

Embed Pmitz in a Java application or run a separate service in your own environment. Your team controls configuration and storage and takes responsibility for hosting, updates, maintenance, and operating costs.

Start the developer setup .

Software license

The self-operated Pmitz software is open source under Apache 2.0 , with no software license fee. Your team covers its own hosting and operating costs. A Pmitz SaaS subscription is a separate paid service from Terpomo Software.

How Pmitz fits alongside billing

Your billing provider can continue collecting payments, producing invoices, and managing the relevant commercial subscription information. Your application supplies that information to its Pmitz integration where needed.

Pmitz focuses on configured feature access, applicable customer limits, and reported usage in the application. This is a choice of product and operating model, not a claim that those capabilities are unique to Pmitz.

Stripe offers billing-linked entitlements , and Chargebee provides feature and entitlement management . Specialist products such as Stigg also address runtime entitlement and usage workflows. Evaluate fit, integration, operating responsibilities, and commercial terms for your requirements.

The comparison does not imply a shipped Stripe or Chargebee connector, a nontechnical policy editor, or a usage analytics dashboard.
