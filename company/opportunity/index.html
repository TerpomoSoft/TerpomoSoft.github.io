Planning scenario from the presentation

Opportunity assumptions

The deck retains a hypothetical support-subscription calculation. Its inputs require validation. This is not Pmitz SaaS pricing, a confirmed support-only package, a revenue forecast, or evidence of demand.

Fixed hypothetical spend

The scenario assumes US$200 per company per month, or US$2,400 per year. This amount is held constant in all three scenarios. It is not an established SaaS price or a willingness-to-pay finding.

Pmitz can be self-operated or used through a Pmitz SaaS subscription from Terpomo Software. This retained support-subscription arithmetic does not define a second confirmed package.

Both starting population and annual growth vary. Every case assumes 100% uptake of the hypothetical subscription. Eligible means hypothetically able to use the software and purchase that imagined subscription, not demonstrated customers.

Calculation

annual spend = starting companies
             x (1 + annual growth)^(year - 2027)
             x US$2,400

Four compounding intervals reach 2031. The unrounded results are approximately US$14.6 million, US$35.1 million, and US$84.0 million, displayed as whole millions in the deck.

What needs evidence

Company populations, growth, uptake, product fit, and willingness to pay all need validation before a commercial forecast can be presented. The scenario does not model acquisition rates, market share, margins, SaaS price, or Pmitz revenue.

No validated company population or demand evidence is supplied for these inputs. They are retained planning assumptions, not a sourced total addressable market.
