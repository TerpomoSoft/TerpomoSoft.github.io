Terpomo Software

Terpomo owns and develops Pmitz and maintains its source and integration guides. We are building around a practical problem - applying a software company's plans, limits, and customer terms when its product is used.

A product a software company can evaluate

Pmitz’s published software and guides provide a starting point for a technical evaluation. The open-source product and Pmitz-operated SaaS are two operating choices around the same product.

Useful candidates already have implemented features whose plan packaging, usage limits, or customer terms change. They may also have existing customer, plan, and usage information they want their integration to retain.

Partner discussions

The presentation proposes a specific next step with Ingram: sharpen the paid offer and introduce one suitable software company.

The requested contribution is to refine the offer for that buyer, make an introduction, and help gather buyer feedback. It is a proposed discussion, not an established distribution, resale, or service-delivery contract. No pilot customer or completed introduction is claimed.

A focused evaluation

  1. Connect one existing feature.
  2. Test included and excluded plan membership.
  3. Check a request inside and outside its applicable limit.
  4. Perform the feature and explicitly report successful use.
  5. Verify the intended family, customer, and period total, then check the next request.
  6. Apply the music example’s changes: add existing family sharing to Free, and increase Premium’s monthly quota from 100 to 200.
  7. Measure the integration and change work, and gather feedback on product fit and proposed paid help.

Concurrency, failures, duplicates, and multi-instance behavior belong in a separate technical evaluation of the complete application flow. This proposed scope is not a completed result or a launch commitment.
