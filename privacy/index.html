Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy covers the Terpomo Software website (terpomosoft.com), the Pmitz documentation site (terpomo-io.github.io/pmitz), the Pmitz software, the Pmitz plugin for AI assistants, and the instant Pmitz servers that Terpomo hosts at pmitz-mcp.web.app. Questions and requests go to contact@terpomosoft.com .

The short version

  • The Pmitz software and the Pmitz plugin do not send telemetry to Terpomo Software. If you connect the plugin to a Pmitz server, it sends requests to the server you select.
  • An instant Pmitz server is hosted by Terpomo Software. What you or your assistant put in it is stored by us until it expires, 72 hours after it was created.
  • Our websites use Google Analytics on the Pmitz pages to count visits. The other pages have no analytics.
  • If you email us, we use your message and address to answer you.
  • We do not sell personal information.

The Pmitz plugin

The plugin includes integration instructions, reference documents, example code and a Docker Compose file. It can work with a Pmitz server in two ways: through a connector that runs on your machine and talks to your own server, and through instant Pmitz servers that Terpomo hosts, described in the next section.

  • The connector runs on your machine and sends requests only to the Pmitz server URL you configure. It sends the API key to that server to authenticate. Claude Code accepts the key as a sensitive plugin setting. In Codex, the connector reads it from the local environment.
  • The connector can read or change product definitions, subscriptions and usage on that server. It returns results, which can include subscription identifiers, plan details and usage counts, to your assistant. The connector does not keep a separate copy of those records.
  • Your assistant provider processes your prompts, code and tool results under its own privacy terms. The plugin does not send a separate copy to Terpomo Software. If Terpomo operates the Pmitz server you select, the service agreement described below applies.
  • If you start the Pmitz server with the bundled Compose file, Docker downloads the server image from Docker Hub. Docker’s own terms apply to that download.

Instant Pmitz servers

An assistant with the Pmitz plugin, or any MCP client, can create an instant Pmitz server at pmitz-mcp.web.app. It is a temporary Pmitz server for trying the product and for development, with no account. Terpomo Software operates this service on Google Cloud, in the United States.

  • What we store. The product definitions, subscription identifiers, plan assignments and usage counts that you or your assistant put in the instance, a one-way hash of the instance key and of its claim code, and when the instance was created and when it expires. We do not ask for your name or email address, and there is no account.
  • What it is for. We use this data only to run the instance: to answer the requests made with its key.
  • How long we keep it. An instance and everything in it are deleted after it expires, 72 hours after it was created. Deletion runs shortly after the expiry time.
  • Request logs. Google Cloud records the requests the service receives, including IP address, time, address requested and response status, and keeps those logs for 30 days. We use them to keep the service running and to stop abuse. The service does not log request or response bodies; an error message in its logs can contain an identifier from the request that failed.
  • What not to put in it. An instance is not meant for personal or confidential information. Use made-up customer identifiers.
  • Your assistant. Your assistant provider processes your prompts and the tool results under its own privacy terms.

The Pmitz software

Pmitz is open-source software that you run yourself, either as a Java library inside your application or as a server in your own environment.

  • Pmitz stores subscriptions and usage counters in the database you configure. That data stays in your environment.
  • Pmitz contains no telemetry and does not report usage, configuration or customer data to Terpomo Software.
  • You are responsible for the personal information your own application stores in Pmitz, such as customer or subscription identifiers.

Pmitz SaaS

When Terpomo Software operates Pmitz for a customer, the data handled in that service is governed by the subscription agreement with that customer. This policy does not replace that agreement.

Our websites

Analytics

The Pmitz pages of terpomosoft.com (the product overview, the product guide, the Pmitz SaaS page and the developer page) and the Pmitz documentation site use Google Analytics. No other page of terpomosoft.com uses it.

On the pages that use it, Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and records:

  • the pages you view and how long you stay on them
  • scrolling, and clicks on links that leave the site
  • the site that referred you
  • your browser, device type and operating system
  • your approximate location, such as city and country, derived from your IP address

We use this to understand how the Pmitz pages are used. We do not use it for advertising. Google processes this data as described in how Google uses information from sites that use its services . You can block it with your browser’s cookie settings or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on .

Hosting

Both sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives the technical information every web request carries, including your IP address, and may keep it in server logs as described in the GitHub privacy statement . We do not receive those logs.

Links to other sites

Our sites link to other services, including GitHub, Docker Hub and Maven Central. Their own policies apply once you follow a link.

When you contact us

If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.

If you open an issue or a discussion on GitHub, what you post there is public. Keep credentials and customer data out of public issues.

Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. The only parties that process it for us are the service providers named above: Google, for analytics and for hosting the instant Pmitz servers, and GitHub, for hosting our websites.

We may disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Google Analytics keeps visit data for no longer than 14 months, after which Google deletes it. An instant Pmitz server and its contents are deleted after it expires, 72 hours after it was created, and the request logs of that service are kept for 30 days. Email correspondence is kept for as long as it is needed for the conversation or for our business records, and is deleted on request.

Your choices and requests

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent to its use. Write to contact@terpomosoft.com . We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we publish the new version on this page and update the effective date.
